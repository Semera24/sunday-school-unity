# Pilot and rollout

Status: proposal; institutional sponsor and participating schools not confirmed.

## Pilot selection

Select a small group with differing sizes/connectivity and active lead/department coordinators. One diocese with roughly 3–5 schools is a planning suggestion, not an agreement. Identify the institutional sponsor, owner of data decisions, support contact and hosting payer before entering real member data.

## Preparation

Collect real blank membership forms, department lists, report templates and approval examples. Confirm organizational hierarchy and lead appointments. Define required member data, import mapping/duplicate review, communication, retention and assisted access. Use fictional data until access and operations are manually accepted.

## Training by responsibility

| Audience | Practice |
|---|---|
| Lead | Membership approvals, department/coordinator setup, local review/release |
| Coordinator | Relevant member records, activity entry, report drafts/corrections |
| Member | Profile, assigned forms, pending state, information access |
| Upper officer | Reporting completeness, released report review, corrections |
| Operator | Deployment, access recovery, backup/restore and support escalation |

## Acceptance and feedback

Owner manually exercises the accepted scenarios in docs/09 and the requirements. Track issues against backlog IDs; distinguish feature implementation from owner acceptance. Include two schools with similar names in one city, a member in several departments, returned report revisions, pending membership access, file/export boundaries and incomplete reporting periods.

## Expansion conditions

Pilot users can complete core work, critical reported issues are resolved, totals reconcile, member information remains appropriately scoped, restore has been demonstrated, and a support/operations owner is available. Review feedback before adding dioceses or specialist modules. No national launch date is promised.
