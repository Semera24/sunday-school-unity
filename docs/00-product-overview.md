# Sunday School Unity — product overview

Status: presentation draft, 14 November 2026. Working name; official branding and sponsor approval pending.

## One-sentence introduction

Sunday School Unity connects Ethiopian Orthodox Sunday Schools through one platform where members maintain their profiles, local coordinators manage departments and activities, and higher offices receive approved reports and coordinate service.

## Problem and intended value

The intended problem is fragmented membership information, departmental records and reporting across many churches and locations. The project aims to make local administration easier while giving coordinating offices consistent, timely information. These are product hypotheses to validate with pilot schools, not measured claims about all existing operations.

| Audience | Intended value |
|---|---|
| Members/disciples | Own profile, assigned forms, relevant information and shared resources |
| Department coordinators | Organized member/activity records and easier report preparation |
| Sunday School leads | Local control, membership approval, coordinator appointments and report approval |
| District/diocese offices | Approved submissions, completeness tracking and consolidated oversight |
| Union board/divisions | Countrywide coordination and authorized functional reporting |

## Product shape

Hierarchy is the heart of the application. Each Sunday School has a separate management workspace. Several churches and Sunday Schools can exist in one city. Local departments and higher-office divisions have their own responsibilities. Explicit shared spaces allow experience and resource exchange without exposing private management records.

The local lead approves member registrations/account links and outgoing reports. Higher offices provide general management and oversight; everyday member approval remains local.

## Proposed MVP

1. Organization hierarchy and local workspaces.
2. Member registration, lead approval, profile and assigned forms.
3. Configurable departments, member assignments and coordinator appointments.
4. Department activity/participation records.
5. Draft, local approval, upward release and review of reports.
6. Scoped dashboards with missing/late-report indicators.
7. Announcements and a bounded resources/experience-sharing area.
8. Amharic/English interface, useful exports and manual acceptance.

Detailed accounting, stock management, examinations, payroll, real-time chat, offline synchronization and electronic elections are not approved MVP commitments.

## Example presentation story

A member submits a profile request to their Sunday School. The lead reviews and approves it. An authorized coordinator records department activity and prepares a period report. The lead approves its release. The district/diocese receives that approved version; national officers view authorized consolidated information and reporting completeness. Separately, a school publishes an approved experience resource to a selected sharing audience.

## Success measures to agree for the pilot

Member registration/approval completion, coordinator task completion, timely report submission, report corrections, support requests and school willingness to continue. Targets require sponsor/pilot input; no adoption or performance results exist yet.
